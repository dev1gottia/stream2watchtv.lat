1. Overview & Policy Statement

stream2watchtv.lat ("we", "our", or "the Platform") respects the intellectual property rights of content creators, sports leagues, broadcasting networks, and copyright owners. It is our strict policy to respond promptly to clear, compliant notices of alleged copyright infringement in accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act of 1998 (17 U.S.C. § 512 et seq.) and international intellectual property treaties.

Important Notice: stream2watchtv.lat acts strictly as an online technology intermediary and content indexing index. We do not host, store, or upload media files directly onto our server hardware.

2. Safe Harbor Compliance

Under Title II of the DMCA (the Online Copyright Infringement Liability Limitation Act), online service providers that meet specific statutory criteria are shielded from monetary liability for copyright infringement.

stream2watchtv.lat qualifies for Safe Harbor protections by maintaining an active, expeditious takedown procedure, having registered a Designated Copyright Agent, and enforcing a strict policy regarding repeated violations.

3. Infringement Notification Procedure

To file a legally binding copyright infringement notification under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), the copyright holder or their authorized legal agent must provide a written communication containing the following mandatory elements:

  1. Physical or Electronic Signature: A signature of the person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  2. Identification of Copyrighted Work: Specific identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (e.g., event broadcast title, match date, league certification).
  3. Identification of Infringing Material: Clear URL(s) or exact location on stream2watchtv.lat where the allegedly infringing material appears, enabling us to locate and expeditiously disable it.
  4. Contact Information: Full legal name, mailing address, telephone number, and active corporate email address.
  5. Good Faith Statement: A statement that the complaining party has a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. Accuracy & Penalty of Perjury Declaration: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that the complaining party is authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
Legal Warning: Under Section 512(f) of the DMCA, any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material or activity is infringing may be subject to substantial liability for damages, including court costs and attorneys' fees.

5. Counter-Notification Procedure

If you believe that your content or link was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification, you may submit a written counter-notification pursuant to Section 512(g)(2) and (3) of the DMCA.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notification, we will promptly forward a copy to the original complaining party. Unless the copyright owner files a court action seeking a restraining order within 10–14 business days, the disabled material may be restored.

6. Repeat Infringer Policy

In compliance with Section 512(i)(1)(A) of the DMCA, stream2watchtv.lat enforces an automated repeat infringer policy. We terminate access privileges and permanently block accounts, IP ranges, or submitters identified as engaging in repeated, willful intellectual property violations.
