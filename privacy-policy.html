1. Introduction & Commitment

At stream2watchtv.lat ("we", "our", or "Platform"), we prioritize the privacy and confidentiality of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we gather when you visit our website, stream live sports broadcasts, purchase VIP passes, or interact with our interactive features, and explains our practices for safeguarding that data.

Privacy Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or viewing habits to commercial third-party advertisers.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver high-quality, buffer-free streaming services:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Account & Email Details: Your email address when registering for access or purchasing VIP Match Passes to deliver your instant authentication token.
  • Customer Support Communications: Any inquiries or messages submitted to our 24/7 support desk or DMCA compliance team.

B. Automated Technical Information

  • Device & Browser Metadata: Browser type, operating system version, screen resolution, and preferred language to optimize 4K video rendering.
  • Stream Performance Telemetry: Bandwidth speed metrics, dropped frames, and CDN edge latency statistics used strictly for adaptive bitrate switching.
  • IP Address & Geolocation: Pseudonymized IP address to connect your player to the closest global CDN server.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is processed exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Uninterrupted Video Delivery: Routing your connection to the lowest-latency edge server (Server 1, 2, or 3).
  • VIP Access Verification: Authenticating active 24-hour, monthly, or season passes across authorized devices.
  • Platform Security & Fraud Prevention: Preventing distributed denial of service (DDoS) attacks and stopping unauthorized account sharing.
  • Continuous Quality Improvement: Analyzing aggregate buffering statistics to optimize video encoding pipelines.

4. Payment Security & Encryption

stream2watchtv.lat implements industry-leading security practices for all payment transactions:

Zero Credit Card Storage: We do NOT store your credit card numbers, CVV security codes, or full banking credentials on our servers. All financial transactions are processed by certified PCI-DSS Level 1 payment gateways using end-to-end 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption.

For cryptocurrency transactions (USDT, Bitcoin), payments are confirmed directly via decentralized blockchain ledgers without requiring personal identity documentation.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential and functional cookies and browser local storage strictly to:

  • Remember your preferred video player volume and audio track preferences.
  • Retain your active VIP session token so you don't need to re-login during live matches.
  • Remember sports filter preferences (e.g. Football vs UFC).

You can configure your browser to reject cookies; however, some features such as stream quality persistence or automatic pass recognition may be impacted.

6. Third-Party CDNs & Infrastructure

To provide instantaneous 60FPS video globally, we partner with reputable enterprise Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) and infrastructure providers. These partners process anonymized network telemetry under strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) that prohibit secondary data usage.

7. GDPR & CCPA User Rights

Under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you possess the following explicit data rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of any personal data associated with your account.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate email records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request immediate deletion of your email and purchase history.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may object to specific telemetry processing activities.

8. Data Retention Policy

We retain transaction reference logs for a period of 90 days following pass expiration to fulfill accounting requirements and handle refund inquiries. Transient streaming telemetry logs are automatically wiped every 14 days.
